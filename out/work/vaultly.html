Overview

A complete UX redesign of a fintech portfolio dashboard — research-led, prototype-tested, and delivered as a production-ready Figma system.

A dashboard that finally made sense.

Vaultly's dashboard had evolved organically over three years of feature additions. The result was a product their power users had memorised — and everyone else couldn't navigate. Support tickets about basic tasks were piling up.

We started with research: 12 user interviews, task analysis, and a heuristic audit that identified 34 distinct usability issues. From there, we rebuilt the information architecture from scratch and redesigned every core screen.

The challenge

Redesigning a complex financial tool without alienating the power users who had built their workflows around the existing interface.

Improving usability without breaking muscle memory.

The core tension was between new users — for whom the product felt overwhelming — and power users who had spent months learning its quirks. Any redesign that helped one group at the expense of the other would create more problems than it solved.

We solved this through progressive disclosure — surfacing the most common actions prominently while keeping advanced functionality accessible but unobtrusive. Power users found everything they needed; new users could get started without a manual.

How we worked

A 7 weeks process built for speed and quality.

Week 01–02
Research
User interviews, task analysis, heuristic audit, analytics review, and competitive benchmarking.
Week 02–03
Architecture
Information architecture redesign, user flow mapping, and card sorting sessions with target users.
Week 03–06
Design & test
Wireframes, visual design, interactive prototype, and two rounds of usability testing.
Week 06–07
Handoff
Production-ready Figma file, component library, design tokens, and developer documentation.
