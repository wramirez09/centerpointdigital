Overview

A Shopify Plus migration and full redesign for a boutique home goods retailer — transforming a dated WooCommerce store into a premium e-commerce experience.

A store worthy of the products it sells.

Fenwick's products were exceptional — handcrafted home goods with a devoted following. Their online store was not. Built on WooCommerce years earlier, it was slow, hard to manage, and presented premium products in a decidedly unpremium environment.

We migrated the entire catalogue to Shopify Plus, redesigned every page with editorial-quality photography guidance, and rebuilt the checkout flow from scratch. The store launched to their best revenue month on record.

The challenge

Migrating a 3,000+ SKU catalogue without disrupting an active retail operation or losing any existing SEO equity.

Migrating at scale without breaking what works.

With over 3,000 SKUs and thousands of product reviews accumulated over years, the migration had to be executed with zero data loss and carefully managed URL redirects to preserve search rankings.

We built a custom migration script that transferred products, variants, metafields, and customer data in a single overnight operation, with a parallel staging environment that was tested exhaustively before cutover.
