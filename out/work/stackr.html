Overview

A high-converting marketing landing page for a developer productivity tool — animated product demos, social proof, and a frictionless sign-up flow.

A landing page that works as hard as the product.

Stackr had a great product but a placeholder landing page that wasn't converting. They needed something that could communicate technical value to a developer audience without feeling corporate or over-designed.

We built a page around the product itself — animated code demos, real benchmark numbers, and a minimal sign-up flow that reduced time-to-action. The result was a 4.1% conversion rate, nearly 3× their previous page.

The challenge

Communicating technical depth to a skeptical developer audience without losing clarity or slowing down the page.

Earning trust from an audience that hates being sold to.

Developers don't respond to marketing speak. Every word on the page had to be backed by something real — a benchmark, a demo, a testimonial from someone they'd recognise.

Performance was non-negotiable. We built the animated demos in pure CSS and vanilla JS to keep the Lighthouse score above 95, and lazy-loaded everything below the fold.
