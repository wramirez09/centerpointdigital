Overview

A website for Sterling Foster Consulting, an ISO 9001 and quality management consultancy in Buffalo Grove, IL, serving manufacturers and regulated organizations.

A credible home for 30 years of quality leadership.

Sterling Foster helps manufacturers prepare for ISO and customer audits, strengthen documentation, and fix the root causes behind quality problems. The site needed to make that expertise easy to understand for operations and quality managers, and make it easy to get in touch.

The site is organized around the work Sterling does: a services overview plus dedicated pages for Internal Audits, Documentation, Corrective Action, Quality, and Quality Support, and an About page covering his ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949 lead auditor credentials. A click-to-call number and a "Schedule a Consultation" button sit in the header on every page, and the consultation form sends inquiries by email with inline success and error messages.

It's a static site built with HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap, with GSAP scroll animations and Swiper carousels, and it's deployed on Cloudflare Pages, so it loads fast and has no server or CMS to maintain.
