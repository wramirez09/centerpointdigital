Overview

A health tracking iOS app built from scratch — from product scoping and UX research through to App Store launch in 8 weeks.

A health app that people actually use.

Pulse Labs had a clear product vision — a simple, beautiful health tracker that got out of the user's way. What they needed was a team who could translate that vision into a native iOS app, fast.

We ran a two-week sprint to define core features, map user flows, and validate the information architecture with real users. Then we built. Eight weeks later, Pulse was live on the App Store.

The challenge

Building a consumer health app that feels effortless — while handling the complexity of HealthKit integration and real-time data visualisation.

Simple on the surface. Complex underneath.

The biggest technical challenge was HealthKit integration — reading from multiple data sources, normalising them, and presenting them in a way that felt instant. We built a local caching layer that made even the first load feel fast.

On the design side, the challenge was restraint. Every feature request was evaluated against one question: does this make the app simpler or more complex for the user? Most were cut.

How we worked

A 8 weeks process built for speed and quality.

Week 01–02
Scoping & research
User interviews, competitor audit, feature prioritisation, and HealthKit API planning.
Week 02–04
UX & visual design
User flows, wireframes, high-fidelity Figma designs, and usability testing with 8 participants.
Week 04–07
Build & test
Swift development, HealthKit integration, real-time data visualisation, and internal QA.
Week 07–08
App Store launch
App Store submission, review management, launch monitoring, and post-launch bug fixes.
