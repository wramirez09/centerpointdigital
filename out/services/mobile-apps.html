Overview

We scope the smallest version worth shipping, then build it to a standard you won’t have to throw away.

From idea to the App Store, without the bloat.

A mobile app is a big investment, and the riskiest part is building the wrong thing. We start by pinning down the core job your app does for users, then design and build a focused first version you can put in front of real people quickly.

We build with native Swift for iOS or React Native when you need both platforms from one codebase, and we connect your app to the same backend, auth, and APIs as your web product so everything stays in sync.

FAQ

Common questions.

Native or cross-platform?

If you’re iOS-first or need deep device features, we recommend native Swift. If you need both platforms on a tighter budget, React Native gets you there with one codebase.

How long until we’re in the App Store?

A focused MVP typically takes eight to twelve weeks from kickoff to store submission.

Can the app share a backend with our website?

Yes — and it usually should. We connect both to the same APIs and accounts so users get one consistent product.

Do you help after launch?

We offer ongoing support for updates, OS releases, new features, and monitoring.
